# Fundamental Technologies

> Fundamental Technologies Inc. (Fundamental) is an AI company building foundation models for predictive decision-making across enterprises and governments. Its flagship product, NEXUS, is a large tabular model pre-trained on billions of real-world enterprise tables, designed to capture the complex non-linear feature interactions in structured data that large language models handle poorly. Founded by DeepMind alumni, the company raised a $255M Series A in February 2026. As of this profile, Fundamental publishes a marketing and research presence but no public developer API, documentation, or SDKs.
